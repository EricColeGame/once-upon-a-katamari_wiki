## What Counts as a Hidden Level in Once Upon A Katamari?

The game is built around nine stages set in different time periods, each populated with inhabitants who hand out requests for Katamaris. Most requests are visible the moment you enter an era, but a meaningful chunk of them stay hidden behind conditions the game tracks silently in the background.

Broadly speaking, Once Upon A Katamari hidden levels fall into four buckets, and each one behaves differently.

| Type of hidden level | How it shows up on the map | What usually unlocks it |
|---|---|---|
| Cousin-gated request | Locked icon with a "X out of Y" counter | Collecting a set number of Cousins |
| Story-gated era | Era simply not present yet | Completing the previous chapter |
| Collectible-triggered request | Extra request appears inside an era | Rolling up specific Crowns or Presents |
| Post-game bonus request | Appears only after the credits | Finishing the main storyline |

One piece of series trivia worth knowing: Once Upon A Katamari is the second game in the franchise built around a time-travel plot, following the Time Trip level pack from Katamari Amore. That structure is exactly why the level list is era-based, and why unlocking one request can quietly open another in a completely different time period.

## Cousin Gates: Why So Many Requests Stay Locked

If a stage looks like it should be playable but refuses to open, Cousins are almost always the reason. Cousins are scattered across every era, and some are tucked behind size requirements, inside buildings, or on top of structures you can only reach once your Katamari is large enough.

Community reports on Steam discussions describe locked requests showing counters such as 25 out of 29 and 35 out of 39, with the Ice Age level cited as a specific example of a stage that stayed sealed despite a large Cousin collection. Those numbers are player-reported rather than officially published, but the pattern is consistent: the game is checking your total Cousin count, not just what you found in that era.

| Reported gate | What players saw | Practical takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| Around 25 Cousins | Ice Age request locked at 25/29 (community reports) | Re-sweep early eras for missed Cousins |
| Around 35 Cousins | Later request locked at 35/39 (community reports) | Target eras with dense Cousin clusters |
| 68 Cousins total | Full collection across all stages | The long-term 100% goal |

The tricky part is that Cousins can be picked up accidentally during normal rolling. You may have collected one without realizing it, which is why the counter can jump unexpectedly after an unrelated run.

## How to Unlock Once Upon A Katamari Hidden Levels, Step by Step

There's no cheat code that flips every lock open — the reliable approach to Once Upon A Katamari hidden levels is methodical progress. Work through these steps in order and you'll clear gates far faster than by rolling randomly.

1. **Finish the main request in your current chapter.** New eras only appear after the previous chapter's core objective is complete.
2. **Replay cleared eras with a bigger Katamari.** Many Cousins sit behind size thresholds you couldn't reach on your first attempt.
3. **Check your Cousin count after every single run.** The number is the gate, so you want to know exactly where you stand.
4. **Sweep for Crowns.** There are 141 of them, and they're often hidden in the same corners as hard-to-reach Cousins.
5. **Grab Presents when you spot them.** They're not the main gate, but they're usually placed near collectibles you actually need.
6. **Revisit early eras last.** Small Cousins are easiest to miss when your Katamari is huge and sweeping everything up.
7. **Recheck the stage select after each unlock.** New requests often appear without any notification at all.

| 2 | Re-roll with a larger Katamari | Reaches size-locked Cousins |

| 5 | Collect Presents | Completion plus Cousin proximity |

## Crowns, Cousins, and Presents: What the Numbers Look Like

Completionists should know the scale of the job up front. According to the widely referenced collectible breakdown, the game contains 248 collectible locations in total, split across three categories. Crowns and Cousins are the ones that matter most for unlocking content and earning trophies, while Presents are largely cosmetic and collection-driven.

| Collectible | Total count | Role in progression |
|---|---|---|
| Crowns | 141 | Hidden throughout stages; required for completion |
| Cousins | 68 | Directly unlock gated requests and levels |
| Presents | 39 | Cosmetic collection and completion tracking |
| **Total** | **248** | The full 100% checklist |

Because Cousins are the gate for so many locked requests, it helps to think of them as keys rather than collectibles. Every Cousin you roll up is potentially one step closer to revealing a stage you didn't even know existed.

## Tips for Finding Hidden Levels Faster

A few habits separate players who clear the map quickly from those who grind the same era endlessly.

- **Play each era twice.** First run for the objective, second run purely for collectibles with a larger Katamari.
- **Use the Cousin list as a to-do list.** If a stage is locked, compare your count against the reported thresholds rather than guessing.
- **Prioritize vertical spaces.** Rooftops, towers, and elevated platforms hide a disproportionate number of Cousins and Crowns.
- **Don't ignore tiny objects.** Some Cousins start small enough that a large Katamari vacuums them up by accident — or misses them entirely because you rolled past.
- **Finish chapters before hunting.** Unlocking a new era often changes which requests are available in old ones.

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Request icon won't open | Cousin threshold not met | Recount Cousins and re-run early eras |
| Era missing from the map | Previous chapter unfinished | Complete the current chapter's main request |
| Stage select looks unchanged | Unlock happened in another era | Scroll through every time period |
| Nothing new after credits | Post-game requests need specific runs | Try large-Katamari runs in cleared eras |

## FAQ

**How many Once Upon A Katamari hidden levels are there?**
The game doesn't officially label any request as "hidden," so the number depends on how you count. There are nine eras, each containing multiple requests, and the locked ones generally break down into Cousin-gated stages, story-gated eras, and post-game bonus requests.

**Why is a level still locked when I have plenty of Cousins?**
Double-check the counter shown on the locked icon. Community reports suggest some gates use cumulative totals that don't match what you think you've collected, and a single missed Cousin in an early era can keep a late-game stage sealed.

**Can you unlock Once Upon A Katamari hidden levels without collecting every Cousin?**
Yes. Most gates require a partial threshold rather than all 68 Cousins, so you can open the majority of locked requests without a full collection. Completing everything, however, does require tracking down the entire set.

**Do Crowns unlock levels too?**
Crowns are primarily completion collectibles rather than unlock keys — Cousins drive most request gates. That said, Crowns are often hidden in the same hard-to-reach spots as the Cousins you need, so hunting them together is the efficient play.
