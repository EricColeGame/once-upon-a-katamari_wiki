Once Upon A Katamari stuffs 248 collectibles into its colorful, chaotic worlds, and 141 of them are Crowns. If you're chasing the Platinum trophy, tracking down every **Once Upon A Katamari crown location** isn't optional — it's the entire assignment. Crowns and Cousins are the only collectible types tied to trophies, so Presents can sit this one out completely.

This guide breaks down the **Once Upon A Katamari crown locations** level by level, covering the sneaky hiding spots, the size requirements that trip players up, and the Freebie powerups that make the hunt far less painful. Whether you're stuck on a crown wedged behind a dartboard or one floating on a shark's back, there's a route for it below.

## Crowns, Cousins, and Presents: What Actually Matters

Before you start rolling, it helps to know exactly what the game expects from you. According to community-compiled collectible data, the breakdown looks like this:

| Collectible Type | Total in Game | Needed for Platinum? |
|---|---|---|
| Crowns | 141 | Yes |
| Cousins | 68 | Yes |
| Presents | 39 | No |
| **Combined Total** | **248** | **209 required** |

The math here is refreshingly simple. You need all 68 Cousins and all 141 Crowns, which adds up to 209 collectibles. Presents are pure bonus content — grab them if you enjoy the hunt, but skipping them won't cost you a single trophy.

There's one more wrinkle worth knowing: collecting every Cousin unlocks the "Tag, You're It!" levels, and each of those stages has its own trophy tied to grabbing the Crowns inside. That means Cousin hunting and Crown hunting are inseparable. You can't finish one without the other.

## Freebie Powerups That Make Crown Hunting Easier

Freebies are the game's temporary powerups, and a few of them are genuinely transformative for collectible runs. Here's how each one behaves:

| Freebie | Effect | Best Use for Crowns |
|---|---|---|
| Magnet | Pulls nearby smaller objects toward you | Grabbing crowns on high ledges or across gaps |
| Sonar | Reveals the closest collectible | Dark stages like Lots of Yokai |
| Rocket | Temporarily boosts rolling speed | Covering ground fast in timed stages |
| Timer | Freezes the clock and moving objects | One-minute stages such as Make a Star Again 1 |

A few practical notes from player experience. The Magnet is the single most valuable Freebie for crown hunting, because it yanks hard-to-reach crowns straight into your Katamari without any precision rolling. The Sonar is a lifesaver in low-visibility stages, though it won't show up as a pickup in most levels until you've beaten and replayed them at least once.

The Rocket and Timer are more situational. The Timer can hold a moving crown in place long enough to grab it, which matters on stages where collectibles ride around on carts, turtles, or boats. One bonus quirk: any Freebie still in hand when you finish a level carries over to the next one. If you spot a Magnet early in a stage you don't need it for, save it.

## Practical Tips for Collecting Every Crown

- **Grab small-space crowns early.** If a crown hides under a ramp, inside a container, or behind furniture, collect it before your Katamari grows too wide to fit.
- **Fail means forfeit.** Collectibles do not save if you quit out or fail a stage. You'll have to re-collect anything you grabbed on that run.
- **Bank a Magnet.** Because Freebies carry between levels, stockpile one from an easy stage and deploy it on a crown that's tormenting you.
- **Expect replay for Cousins.** Cousins spawn sequentially within a level, so stages with multiple Cousins require multiple runs.
- **Use Sonar in the dark.** Lots of Yokai is genuinely hard to navigate, and Sonar turns a blind search into a straight line.
- **Check rooftops and ladders.** A huge number of crowns sit on top of saloons, pagodas, and wooden structures reachable only by climbing.

## FAQ

**How many crown locations are there in Once Upon A Katamari?**

There are 141 crowns spread across the game's stages, typically three per level. Alongside 68 Cousins, they form the 209 collectibles required for the Platinum trophy.

**Do I need every Once Upon A Katamari crown location for the Platinum?**

Yes. Crowns and Cousins are the only collectibles tied to trophies. Presents are optional, so you can skip all 39 of them without affecting your Platinum run.

**Why can't I find a crown that should be on a level?**

The most common causes are size restrictions and moving collectibles. Some crowns only appear or become reachable at a specific Katamari size, while others ride around on carts, boats, or animals and need to be intercepted.

**Do collectibles save if I quit a level?**

No. If you quit out or fail a stage, any collectibles gathered during that attempt are lost. Finish the level to lock in your progress.
