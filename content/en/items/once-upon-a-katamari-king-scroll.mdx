## What Is the Once Upon A Katamari King Scroll?

The Once Upon A Katamari king scroll — usually called the Cosmos Scroll in wiki entries and story summaries — is the ancient scroll the King of All Cosmos stumbles across while his family is busy cleaning. It is not a weapon, a power-up, or a stage key. It is a prop with consequences, and in this entry it happens to be the object that sets the entire plot in motion.

Players first meet it in a cutscene. The King, being as flamboyant as ever, decides to show off by spinning the scroll around. Within moments the Earth, the Moon, and a wide band of stars are gone. Everything you roll afterward is an attempt to put the sky back together.

That double life is exactly what makes the Once Upon A Katamari king scroll so confusing to research. It is a story object during cutscenes and a reward-track object inside menus, and the community uses at least three different names for the pieces of it.

### The scroll objects players actually talk about

| Scroll-related object | What it is | Where you encounter it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmos Scroll | The ancient scroll the King finds during the royal family's cleaning day | Story cutscenes, the sky, later sequences | A meteor tears a hole through it, and it begins swallowing stars |

The practical takeaway: when someone says "King Scroll," they almost always mean the gallery illustration. When someone says "Cosmos Scroll," they mean the thing that broke the universe.

## The Story: How One Scroll Broke the Cosmos

The setup is deliberately silly, which is the point. The King finishes a task, gets bored, and starts playing with the scroll instead of putting it away. The scroll goes up into the air. The Earth, the Moon, and the stars do not survive the landing.

Community write-ups describe the same chain of events with slightly different emphasis. One version has the King simply tossing the scroll and crushing the planets outright. Another has a large meteor tearing a hole through the Cosmos Scroll, which then begins sucking in stars. Either way the result is identical: a broken sky and a Prince who has to roll it back.

| Beat | What happens | Who's involved | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleaning day | The royal family tidies up the cosmos | The King and his family | Establishes the mundane setup |
| The discovery | The King finds an old scroll | The King | Introduces the game's central object |
| The accident | The scroll is thrown and spun; planets and stars are destroyed | The King | Creates the game's premise |

| The rebuild | The King restores Earth, then rolls the Cosmos Scroll onward | The King, the Prince | Frames your rolling as repair work |

Notice how little of this is under your control. The Once Upon A Katamari king scroll is not something you chase across a stage or pick up with your katamari. It is something you watch happen, then spend the rest of the game cleaning up after.

### Why the King's boredom matters

The King's boredom is the engine of the whole game. A ruler who is entertained does not throw an irreplaceable artifact into the sky. His restlessness is what turns a routine cleaning job into a planetary emergency — and it is also what gives the story its light, comedic tone instead of a grim one.

## Cosmos Scroll vs. King Scroll vs. Select Scroll

Naming is the biggest source of confusion for new players. Three different scrolls show up in conversation, and only one of them is something you can actually collect. Here is how to tell them apart before you waste hours hunting the wrong thing.

| Name you'll see | Used by | Refers to | Should you chase it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmos Scroll | Wiki entries, story summaries | The in-story scroll | No — it's narrative only |
| King Scroll | Community threads, gallery talk | The illustration reward | Yes, if you're a completionist |
| Select Scroll | Players discussing menus | Stage-select cosmetics | Passive — it upgrades as you play |
| Scroll decorations | Community reports | Extra art on the select screen | Yes, they double as progress markers |

If you searched for "Once Upon A Katamari king scroll" expecting a hidden item in a stage, that is the mix-up. There is no pickup with that name sitting in a corner of a level. The name refers to artwork and story content that unlocks through play, not to a physical object you roll over.

## How to Unlock the Full King Scroll Image

Unlocking scroll content is less about skill and more about persistence. Community reports suggest crown totals are the main gate, so most of your effort goes into replaying stages efficiently rather than mastering a single hard challenge.

| Step | What to do | Notes |
|---|---|---|

### The save-data unlock worth knowing about

There is one scroll-adjacent unlock that has nothing to do with crowns. According to [Bandai Namco's official Once Upon A Katamari news post](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/katamari/news/what-do-you-need-to-know-about-once-upon-a-katamari), players who have save data from *Katamari Damacy REROLL* or *We Love Katamari REROLL + Royal Reverie* can unlock the Young King cousin character. If you already own either of those, boot them once before starting so the save registers.

## Tips for Completing Your Scroll Collection

Crown hunting in a Katamari game is a rhythm game disguised as a collection game. The players who finish fastest are not the ones with the best reflexes — they are the ones who know where the dense clusters of items are and stop wasting time in empty space.

| Tip | Why it helps | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Roll a size target before hunting | Bigger katamaris reach denser item clusters where crowns hide | Medium |
| Replay early stages last | You already know the layouts, so runs go faster | Low |
| Watch for the crown shape, not just the item | Crowns are easy to roll straight past in a crowd | Low |
| Use the select screen as a tracker | Decorations change as you unlock, giving free feedback | None |
| Keep runs short and repeatable | Consistent short runs beat one long, sloppy attempt | Low |

A few habits worth building:

- **Stop treating the scroll as a collectible.** It is cutscene content. Your real checklist is the gallery and the select screen.
- **Check the select screen after every session.** Scroll decorations update quietly, so it is easy to miss progress.
- **Do not restart a run over one missed crown.** Finish the stage, bank the total, and grab the stray item on the next pass.
- **Ask the community when numbers look off.** Crown thresholds are widely discussed and updated by players far more often than by official documentation.

The reward for all of this is cosmetic. The full Once Upon A Katamari king scroll image does not change how the Prince rolls, how fast he moves, or how stages are scored. It is a trophy for people who like seeing a gallery fill up — and if that describes you, it is worth the grind.

## FAQ

**What is the Once Upon A Katamari king scroll?**

It is the ancient scroll the King of All Cosmos finds while his family is cleaning, and it is also the community name for the illustration reward tied to the King. The scroll itself is a story object; the image is a gallery unlock earned through crown collection.

**Does the king scroll change gameplay in any way?**

No. Collecting scroll-related content is purely cosmetic. It does not alter rolling speed, katamari size limits, stage difficulty, or scoring. The only gameplay-adjacent unlock in this family of content is the Young King cousin, which is tied to save data from earlier REROLL titles rather than to crowns.

**How many crowns do you need for the full King Scroll image?**

Community reports put the milestone at roughly 60 crowns, with the illustration described as the remaining reward after that point. Because this comes from player experience rather than an official chart, verify against current community threads before committing to a long grind.

**Can I unlock scroll content without replaying stages?**

Not really. Story progression opens the relevant cutscenes and menu options, but the scroll artwork and decorations require crowns, and crowns require rolling. Save-data unlocks are the one exception, and those depend on owning *Katamari Damacy REROLL* or *We Love Katamari REROLL + Royal Reverie*.

**Is the Cosmos Scroll the same thing as the King Scroll?**

They overlap in conversation but are not identical. The Cosmos Scroll is the in-story object the King throws, and the King Scroll usually refers to the reward artwork. If you are hunting for something to collect, you want the artwork.
