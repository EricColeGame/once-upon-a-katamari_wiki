Presents are the most overlooked collectible in the game, and while they are not tied to a single trophy, they are the reason plenty of players keep replaying stages. ## Why Presents Are Worth Chasing

Unlike crowns, which feed directly into trophies, and cousins, which unlock the *Tag, You're It!* stages, presents are pure bonus content. In Once Upon A Katamari they hide cosmetic accessories for the Prince and his extended family, so grabbing one changes how your character looks while you roll. That makes them optional — and also the easiest thing to forget on a first playthrough.

The good news is that presents are forgiving. You can collect them on any run, in any order, and they are not affected by the sequential cousin spawn rule that forces repeat visits to certain stages. The bad news is that a handful of them are genuinely awkward: lodged in trees, riding minecarts, drifting downstream, or orbiting a whirlpool while your timer bleeds out.

One rule matters more than any other: **collectibles only count once you finish the stage.** Quit out or fail, and every crown, cousin, and present you grabbed on that run is gone. If you are hunting a single stubborn present, finish the stage even if you have to ignore everything else.

## Freebies: The Toolbox for Presents

Freebies are the powerups scattered around stages, and two of them are basically built for present hunting. Here is what each one does and when it earns its slot.

| Freebie | Effect | Best Use for Presents |
|---|---|---|
| Magnet | Pulls objects smaller than your Katamari toward you for a limited time | Presents stuck in trees, nests, and high ledges |
| Sonar | Points you toward the closest collectible, including presents | Dark stages like *Lots of Yokai*, or any time you are lost |
| Timer | Freezes the level clock and all moving objects | Presents riding minecarts, plates, and whirlpools |
| Rocket | Boosts your rolling speed for a limited time | Crossing large stages before the clock runs out |

Two details are easy to miss. First, a Freebie you are still holding when a stage ends carries over into the next stage you play, so you can bank a Magnet for a stage that does not have one. Second, community reports note that the Sonar will not appear as a pickup in most stages until you have beaten and replayed them — so do not plan your first run around it.

## Pro Tips for a Cleaner Presents Run

- **Bank your Freebies.** Finish a stage while still holding a Magnet and it follows you into the next one. That is often the difference between a two-minute grab and a ten-minute headache.
- **Match your size to the present.** Some presents sit under low structures, like the fish rack on the Ghost Ship stage, and you simply will not fit if you have grown too large.
- **Expect the Sonar to be missing early.** It generally will not spawn until you have cleared and replayed a stage, so do not rely on it for a first pass.
- **Do not quit mid-stage.** Failing or quitting wipes your pickups for that run.
- **Skip them guilt-free if you are trophy hunting.** Presents are not part of the Platinum requirement, so there is no penalty for leaving all 39 behind.

## FAQ

**Are Once Upon A Katamari present locations marked on the map?**
No. The game does not flag presents on your map or HUD. Your options are exploration, prior knowledge, or the Sonar Freebie, which points toward the nearest collectible once you have unlocked it by replaying a stage.

**Do I lose a present if I fail or quit a stage?**
Yes. Collectibles are only banked when you complete the stage. If you grab a present and then fail, you will need to pick it up again on the next run.

**Which present is the hardest to get?**
Community reports consistently point to the tree presents, the UFO beam present on *Cowbear Pasture*, and the Santa room present on *Lots of Icicles*. All three depend on timing or a specific Freebie rather than raw rolling skill.

**Do I need presents for the Platinum trophy?**
No. Only crowns and cousins are required. That said, if you are chasing a complete Once Upon A Katamari present locations checklist for collection's sake, all 39 are obtainable in a single dedicated run per stage.

Whether you grab them for the accessories or simply because an empty checklist bothers you, presents are the most relaxed collectible hunt in the game. Load up on Magnets, watch your size, and finish every stage you start — that is the entire strategy.
